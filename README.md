# Sprightly Trade International — Premier C&F Agency in Bangladesh



## About Sprightly Trade International

**Sprightly Trade International** is a premier **C&F Agency (Customs Clearing & Forwarding) in Bangladesh**, providing expert **customs clearance**, **freight forwarding**, and **B2B logistics** services. ## Our Services

### Customs Clearance in Bangladesh
Expert handling of **Import/Export Bill of Entry processing**, bonded warehouse clearance, and port formalities. Our experienced team minimizes customs delays and ensures full regulatory compliance for your shipments entering or leaving Bangladesh.

### Freight Forwarding
Reliable logistics network for cargo clearance at **Dhaka Airport** and sea freight via **Chattogram Port**. We manage the complete freight forwarding process, from documentation to final delivery, ensuring your cargo reaches its destination efficiently.

### Regulatory Compliance
Full adherence to **logistics compliance Bangladesh** laws, ensuring zero-penalty processing. We stay updated on all regulatory changes to protect your business from compliance risks and penalties.

### Logistics Consulting
Expert guidance on trade tariffs, export documentation services, **HS Code classification**, and duty exemptions. Our consultants help you navigate complex trade regulations to maximize cost savings and operational efficiency.
